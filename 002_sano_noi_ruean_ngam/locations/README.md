## Prompts
ทุกภาพ: `Wide cinematic establishing shot, 16:9, no people, no characters, no text.` + รายละเอียดด้านล่าง + Style Base

### ROMWISAI_PALACE
```
The royal palace of Romwisai in an ancient Thai fairy-tale kingdom: ivory-white and gold multi-tiered Thai palace halls with golden chofa finials, a wide courtyard lined with flowering yellow sesbania trees, sesbania motifs carved on gables, lotus ponds, warm golden morning light, gentle and safe atmosphere.
```

### ROMWISAI_SHRINE
```
A small sacred shrine hall inside the Romwisai palace at night: gilded lacquered pillars, mural paintings of sesbania flowers, rows of candles, and on a golden altar at the center a tiny enchanted Thai house carved from pale gold sesbania wood glowing softly with warm amber light (local glow only), hushed sacred mood.
```

### FOREST
```
A deep ancient tropical forest between two Thai kingdoms: giant fig trees with hanging roots, ferns, misty green air, shafts of golden light piercing the canopy, a narrow footpath, mysterious but not frightening.
```

### NOPPARAT_THRONE
```
The grand throne hall of the kingdom of Nopparat: vermilion-red lacquered columns with gold stencil patterns, deep indigo and gold mural walls, a tall gilded busabok throne on a raised platform, tall windows casting daylight beams across polished teak floors, imposing and formal.
```

### SEVEN_CURTAINS
```
A royal funeral chamber with seven layers of sheer translucent curtains in ivory and pale gold hanging one behind another, at the far center a golden funeral bier seen only as a silhouette through the curtains, hundreds of candles casting soft shadows on the layered fabric, secretive and sacred.
```

### BATH_PAVILION
```
A small open teak bathing pavilion on steps leading down into a still palace pond behind the funeral hall at night, moonlight and a few oil lamps, cool indigo palette, lotus leaves on the water, quiet and private.
```

### SERVANT_QUARTERS
```
Humble servants' quarters at the back of the Nopparat palace: weathered wooden stilt houses, an open workspace with banana leaves and flowers for making baisri offerings, clay pots of natural dye and freshly dyed silk drying on bamboo poles in vivid colors, warm afternoon light.
```

### HARBOR
```
The royal harbor of Nopparat at morning: stone steps and gilded pavilions on the riverbank, a magnificent golden royal barge with a carved swan-naga prow and a red silk pennant tied at the bow, calm blue water, bright sky.
```

### SEA_STORM
```
A violent storm at sea in an ancient fairy tale: towering silver-grey waves, dark indigo sky split by lightning, rain sheets, a distant small golden royal barge struggling on the waves, dramatic and powerful.
```

### GIANT_CITY
```
The city of the giants (yak) on a rocky coast: massive black stone walls and towers carved with fierce giant faces, a tall lonely stone tower with a single high window, blood-red banners, thick fog, dim ominous light — the only dark place in the story.
```

### HERMITAGE
```
A hermit sage's hermitage on a green island: a simple thatched sala on stilts, a meditation stone, prayer flags, jade-green jungle and a white sand path, soft golden morning light, peaceful and timeless.
```

### TWIN_PONDS
```
Two small magical ponds side by side next to a forest hermitage: the left pond glowing liquid gold-yellow with soft light rising from it, the right pond glossy pitch-black and perfectly still like oil, separated by a narrow mossy stone path, jade-green jungle around, strongest contrast of gold and black.
```

### RIVER
```
A wide calm river at dawn: silver-gold light on the water, mist drifting over the surface, reeds and sesbania bushes on the banks, a distant island with a hermitage, quiet and hopeful.
```

### BORDER_VILLAGE
```
A poor village on the border of the Romwisai kingdom: small thatched stilt houses, dusty earth paths, rice straw stacks, water buffalo pen, faded earth and straw colors under harsh midday sun, the humble house of an old couple in front.
```

### WILAI_HOUSE
```
The house of a rich village heir: a large polished teak stilt mansion showing off wealth, gaudy gilded ornaments, purple silk curtains, carved verandas, a courtyard with ornamental plants, warm afternoon light, slightly vulgar opulence.
```

### RUEAN_SANO_INTERIOR
```
The impossible interior of a tiny enchanted Thai house, far larger inside than outside: soaring pillars of pale gold sesbania wood covered in gold leaf, a ceiling painted with real twinkling stars in lai rod nam patterns, glowing yellow sesbania blossoms floating in the air like little lanterns, a still pool in the center reflecting golden light, flower-shaped light falling through pierced fretwork, warm, safe, like a mother's embrace.
```
